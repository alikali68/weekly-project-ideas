# SoloBill — invoicing and cash-flow tracking for freelancers paid in crypto

> **One-line pitch:** A lightweight invoicing app and income dashboard for solo freelancers who get paid in a mix of fiat and crypto (USDT/USDC), so they always know what's owed, what's paid, and what the month actually looks like.

## Problem & target users

Solo freelancers rarely have one clean source of truth for money. Invoices live in Google Docs or platform DMs (LaborX, direct clients, bounty programs), payment status is tracked in a spreadsheet or in memory, and crypto payouts sit in wallets disconnected from any accounting. Come tax time — or just the end of the month — there's no reliable answer to "how much did I actually earn, and who still owes me?"

**Target users:** solo freelancers and indie contractors, especially those working internationally and receiving part of their income in stablecoins (USDT/USDC on Tron, Solana, Ethereum). Secondary: bounty hunters and gig workers who stack many small payouts from different sources.

## Tech stack

- **Next.js 14 (App Router) + TypeScript** — one codebase for the marketing site and the app; fastest path to a deployed, SEO-friendly product for a solo dev.
- **Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui** — professional, accessible UI components out of the box; lets design skills shine on the dashboard instead of on boilerplate.
- **PostgreSQL (Neon) + Drizzle ORM** — serverless Postgres with a generous free tier and fully type-safe queries, no heavyweight ORM config.
- **Auth.js (NextAuth)** — email + OAuth login without building auth from scratch.
- **CoinGecko free API** — converts crypto amounts to USD for unified income reporting; cached hourly to stay inside rate limits.
- **React-PDF (@react-pdf/renderer)** — generates clean, branded invoice PDFs server-side.
- **Resend** — transactional email for invoice links and overdue reminders.
- **Vercel (+ Vercel Cron)** — hosting, preview deploys, and a scheduled job for payment reminders.

## Architecture overview

Single Next.js monolith, deployed on Vercel:

- `app/(marketing)` — landing page, pricing, changelog.
- `app/(app)` — authenticated dashboard: overview, invoices, clients, wallets, settings.
- **Server Actions** handle all mutations (create invoice, mark paid, add client); the client never touches the database directly.
- **Vercel Cron** runs nightly: finds invoices past due, sends polite reminder emails via Resend, and flags them in the UI.
- **Wallet sync worker** (a serverless function on a 6h schedule): polls public balances for saved wallet addresses via chain explorers (Etherscan / Solscan APIs) and stores snapshots for the cash-flow chart. No private keys ever touch the system — read-only addresses only.
- Invoice PDFs are rendered on demand by a route handler and cached in blob storage (Vercel Blob); shareable payment links are signed URLs that expire after 30 days.

## MVP feature list (prioritized)

1. **Clients** — add/edit/archive clients with name, email, default currency, and notes.
2. **Invoices** — create invoices with line items, quantities, rates, tax/discount fields, and currency choice (USD, EUR, USDT, USDC).
3. **PDF + shareable link** — one click generates a branded PDF and a public payment link with bank/wallet payment instructions.
4. **Status pipeline** — draft → sent → viewed → paid / overdue, with automatic overdue detection.
5. **Smart reminders** — configurable reminder schedule (e.g. 3 days before due, on due date, 7 days after); email sent via Resend with the invoice attached.
6. **Income dashboard** — monthly bar chart of paid invoices, fiat/crypto split donut, "outstanding" total with per-client breakdown; all crypto converted to USD at payment-time rate.
7. **Wallet address book** — save read-only wallet addresses per chain; dashboard shows live balances next to invoiced income.
8. **CSV export** — one-click export of the year's invoices and payments for the accountant / tax filing.

Explicitly out of MVP: automatic payment reconciliation (v2), multi-user teams, mobile app, invoicing in local languages.

## Data model

- **User** — `id`, `email`, `name`, `business_name`, `logo_url`, `default_currency`, `timezone`, `plan` (free/pro)
- **Client** — `id`, `user_id`, `name`, `email`, `company`, `country`, `default_currency`, `payment_instructions` (text: bank details or wallet address), `archived_at`
- **Invoice** — `id`, `user_id`, `client_id`, `number` (e.g. `SB-2026-014`), `status` (draft/sent/viewed/paid/overdue/cancelled), `currency`, `issue_date`, `due_date`, `notes`, `pdf_url`, `public_token`, `sent_at`, `paid_at`
- **InvoiceLineItem** — `id`, `invoice_id`, `description`, `quantity`, `unit_price`, `position`
- **Payment** — `id`, `invoice_id`, `amount`, `currency`, `method` (bank/crypto/manual), `tx_hash` (nullable, for crypto), `rate_to_usd`, `paid_on`
- **Wallet** — `id`, `user_id`, `label`, `chain` (tron/solana/ethereum), `address`, `last_balance`, `last_synced_at`
- **Reminder** — `id`, `invoice_id`, `scheduled_for`, `sent_at`, `template` (before_due/on_due/overdue)

## Build roadmap (4 weeks)

- **Week 1 — Foundation:** Next.js + Tailwind + shadcn scaffold, Auth.js login, Neon Postgres + Drizzle schema for User/Client/Invoice/LineItem, clients CRUD, invoice CRUD with line items. Deploy to Vercel on day 2 and keep it green.
- **Week 2 — Invoicing core:** invoice numbering, React-PDF generation, public signed payment links, status pipeline, email sending via Resend, reminder scheduler (Vercel Cron) with the three templates.
- **Week 3 — Money view:** payments table with crypto tx_hash + rate_to_usd, CoinGecko conversion service with caching, income dashboard (monthly bars, fiat/crypto donut, outstanding list), CSV export.
- **Week 4 — Wallets + polish:** wallet address book, read-only balance sync worker, landing page + pricing page, onboarding flow (business profile → first client → first invoice), free/pro plan gates, README + demo video. Soft-launch on X and relevant freelance communities.

## Monetization angle

Freemium with a natural upgrade trigger: the free plan covers 5 active invoices per month and 1 wallet — enough to feel the value, tight enough that any working freelancer outgrows it. **Pro at $9/month** (or $90/year) unlocks unlimited invoices, unlimited wallets, custom invoice branding (logo, colors, footer), and automated reminder sequences. A $149 lifetime deal for the first 200 users funds initial traction and creates evangelists. Later expansion: 1% optional fee on "get paid via SoloBill" payment links, and an accountant-seat add-on.
